Title: Community Reading Group

----

Welcome: Welcome to CRG, a tertulia for folks dedicated to becoming more thoughtful about the vital terms that animate appeals to community.

----

Meeting-info: Our primary study group meets every Sunday over Zoom from 7pm to 9pm, Pacific Time. For folks in other time zones, we also hold a regular rotation of satellite groups at different times via our Discord channel.

----

Newsletter-email: communityreadinggroup@gmail.com

----

Workshopintro:

We invite scholars whose work bridges university research and community organizing to submit original, unpublished work to our Workshop Series.

Events can be public or small-group discussions based on the author's preference.

We welcome work intersecting with our primary focus—the rhetorical construction of community—or our peripheral lines of inquiry (see our semantic network graph). We're especially interested in in-progress articles and chapters.

To submit, email communityreadinggroup@gmail.com with the subject line Workshop Submission. Please include your name, a brief bio, the title of your piece, and a short description.
