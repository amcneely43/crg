Etymology: From Old French <em>citeain</em>, from Latin <em>civitas</em> (city-state, citizenship), from <em>civis</em> (citizen). The citizen is the political animal domesticated — the member of a polity who bears rights and duties within a defined legal order. Where the neighbor is proximate, the citizen is juridical.
