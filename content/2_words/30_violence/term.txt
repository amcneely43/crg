Etymology: From Latin <em>violentia</em>, from <em>vis</em> (force, power). Neither simply negative nor positive — violence is the contested medium through which power is enacted, contested, and transformed. In Fanon, violence is the generative force by which the colonized reclaim subjecthood; in its doxa form, it is pure negation and elimination. Its position at neutral valence names this irresolvable tension.
