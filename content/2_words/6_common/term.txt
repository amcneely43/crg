Etymology: From Latin <em>communis</em> (common, public, shared), from <em>com-</em> (together) + <em>munis</em> (under obligation). The root shared with <em>community</em> itself: what is common is what is held under mutual obligation. The common names the prior condition — the shared without ownership — from which community is made and unmade.
