Etymology: From Latin <em>collectivus</em>, from <em>colligere</em> (to gather together), from <em>com-</em> (together) + <em>legere</em> (to pick up). A collective is a gathering held as a unit — the political form of assembly that stops short of fusion and insists on internal difference.
