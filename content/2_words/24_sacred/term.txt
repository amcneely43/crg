Etymology: From Latin <em>sacer</em> (set apart, holy), the root shared with <em>sacrifice</em> and <em>sacrament</em>. The sacred names what a community places beyond ordinary exchange — what is held inviolable not because it is divine, but because the group has collectively agreed to treat it so.
