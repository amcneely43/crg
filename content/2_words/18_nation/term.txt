Etymology: From Latin <em>natio</em> (birth, origin, breed, nation), from <em>nasci</em> (to be born). Nation binds political community to birth — to the accident of origin rather than the choice of association. Its modern career runs from natural to cultural to ethnic to civic, never settling the question of what kind of birth confers belonging.
