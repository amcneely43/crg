Etymology: From Old English <em>fær</em> (calamity, sudden danger). In Hobbes, fear is the originary political affect — the war of all against all that makes community necessary. For Esposito, fear is not the opposite of community but its negative foundation: the munus begins where bare life fears its own annihilation. Doxa renders fear as threat to be escaped; CRG locates it as the ground from which the political emerges.
