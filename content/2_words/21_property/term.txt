Etymology: From Old French <em>propriété</em>, from Latin <em>proprietas</em> (ownership, peculiarity), from <em>proprius</em> (one's own). Property names the boundary between the self and the common — what is mine against what is ours. Its political career runs from natural right to enclosure to capital.
