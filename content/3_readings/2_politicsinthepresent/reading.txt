Note: <p><strong><em>immunity (n.)</em></strong></p><p><strong>late 14c.</strong>, "exemption from service or obligation," from Old French <em>immunité</em> "privilege; immunity from attack, inviolability" (14c.) and directly from Latin <em>immunitatem</em> (nominative <em>immunitas</em>) "exemption from performing public service or charge, privilege," from <em>immunis</em> "exempt, free, not paying a share."
